How it works

Three moves from you, the rest is on us

Say what you have, log what you spend, check the number. That's the whole job.

Keep an eye on your spending

See exactly how much you can still spend, with simple, intuitive counters that update in real time.

You decide the budget

You set the budget for whatever period you want. The math — day by day, week by week — is on iCanSaveit.

Forgiving by design

Forgot to log an expense? No problem: you can always fix the numbers later.

What's under the hood

One calculation, redone every day...

You have €900 for 30 days: today's cap is €30. Spend €12 on lunch? What's left — €888 — gets split again across the 29 days remaining. Tomorrow's cap isn't decided in advance: it's born from what you've spent so far.

...to see where you're headed

iCanSaveit looks at how much you've spent on average per day since you started, and projects it across the days left: so you know today whether you'll have something left over at month's end or you're overspending.
