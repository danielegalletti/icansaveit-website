Frequently asked questions

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes. iCanSaveit is built to work fully offline: it doesn't need the internet during setup or day-to-day use.

I forgot to log an expense — what do I do?

No problem: you can add it at any time, even with a past date. iCanSaveit automatically recalculates your daily/weekly budget to account for it.

What are “planned expenses”?

These are expenses you already know are coming (like a subscription or rent) that you log in the app but that are excluded from the daily budget calculation: they're subtracted directly from your totals, without skewing the average of your daily spending.

What is “extra income”?

It's an amount that increases your available budget instead of reducing it — for example a refund or unexpected income. You log it like any other entry, with the amount entered as negative.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays exclusively on your device. iCanSaveit never sends any information to external servers: you'll find all the details in the Privacy Policy.

If I lose my phone, do I lose my data too?

iCanSaveit itself doesn't back up to any cloud. But if you've enabled iCloud or iTunes backup on your iPhone, the app's data may already be included in that backup, managed directly by Apple.

How do I report a problem or suggest an idea?

Write to us at support@icansaveit.com: we read every message and love hearing what you'd like to see in future versions.
