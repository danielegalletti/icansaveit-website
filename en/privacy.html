Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

In short: iCanSaveit doesn't collect, transmit, or share any data. The app works entirely offline and all the information you enter stays only on your device.

No data collection

iCanSaveit doesn't require creating an account, doesn't connect to any server, and doesn't integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising tools. We don't know who you are, we don't know how you use the app, and we can't know, because no information ever leaves your iPhone.

Where your data is stored

Budgets, expenses, dates, and settings are saved exclusively locally, in the app's database on your device (CoreData). No data is sent to external servers or to cloud services run by iCanSaveit, simply because those servers don't exist.

Permissions and device sensors

iCanSaveit doesn't request access to the camera, location, contacts, photos, or biometric data (Face ID/Touch ID). The app uses the device's motion sensor solely for a small visual effect in the interface: this data is never saved, recorded, or transmitted in any way.

Backups and data deletion

If you uninstall the app, all local data is permanently deleted. If you've enabled iCloud or iTunes backup on your iPhone, the app's data may be included in that backup: this is a feature of the operating system managed directly by Apple, not a service offered by iCanSaveit.

Changes to this policy

If the app introduces features in the future that require different data handling, this page will be updated with the most recent revision date at the top.

Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, you can write to us at privacy@icansaveit.com.
